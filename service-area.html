Drainage systems

What Drainage Systems Do We Install Across Greater New Orleans?

We install the same five drainage systems in every city we serve: French drains, catch basins, channel drains, dry wells and sump pumps. We assess your soil, slope and where the water collects first, then recommend the system that moves it away from your home and yard.

French Drains

A perforated pipe in a gravel-filled trench that carries water away from your foundation.

French drain installation

Catch Basins

A grated collection box set in a low spot, where water gathers and leaves through a pipe.

Catch basin installation

Channel Drains

A shallow trench with a removable grate that catches sheet flow on driveways and patios.

Channel drain installation

Dry Wells

An underground pit of gravel or crushed stone that lets runoff soak slowly into the soil.

Dry well installation

Sump Pumps

A pump in a sump pit at the lowest point of the home that moves water out and away.

Sump pump installation

Problems we fix

Which Yard Drainage Problems Do We Fix Near New Orleans?

We fix standing water on the lawn, yard flooding after heavy rain, water near the foundation and soil that will not drain. In each city we find where your water goes, then install the system that moves it away, from a downspout catch basin to a full French drain.

Yard Flooding After Heavy Rain

Excessive rainfall can flood a yard faster than the ground can take it.

Fixed with catch basins

Standing Water on the Lawn

Water that sits on the lawn can damage your plants and grass.

Fixed with channel drains

Water Near the Foundation

Surface water that collects near your foundation can cause structural damage over time.

Fixed with French drains

Soil That Does Not Drain

Poor quality or compacted soil holds water instead of letting it soak away.

Fixed with dry wells

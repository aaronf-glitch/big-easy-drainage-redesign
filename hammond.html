Common problems

What Causes Drainage Problems in Hammond Yards?

In Hammond the usual causes are clogged gutters and downspouts, land graded toward the house instead of away from it, and drainage systems that are inadequate or failing. Each one lets water pool around the foundation or in low spots, which leads to water damage, mold growth and costly repairs.

Clogged Gutters and Downspouts

Water that cannot flow away pools around the foundation or damages the exterior.

Improper Grading

Land that slopes the wrong way sends water toward the house or into low spots.

Failing Drainage

An inadequate or broken system lets water build up and damage structures and plants.

How each system works

Which Drainage System Fits a Hammond Property?

It depends on where the water collects. A French drain diverts water through a gravel trench, a channel drain catches runoff on driveways and patios, a dry well lets water soak into poorly draining ground, and a sump pump lifts water out of a pit once it rises.

Water Along the House

A French drain catches it in a gravel trench and diverts it away from the property.

Runoff on Hard Surfaces

A channel drain's grate collects it and a pipe leads it off the driveway or patio.

Ground That Stays Wet

A dry well stores it underground, or a sump pump lifts it out of a pit.

About Hammond, LA

Why Should Hammond, LA Homeowners Plan for Heavy Rain?

Hammond is the largest city in Tangipahoa Parish, about 45 miles from both Baton Rouge and New Orleans. When Hurricane Ida's eastern eyewall struck the city in August 2021, it dropped more than 12 inches of rain and caused severe flash flooding, so planned drainage matters here.

Named for a Settler

Hammond is named for Peter Hammond, a Swedish immigrant who settled here around 1818.

Railroad Town

FAQ

Drainage Questions From Hammond Homeowners

These are the questions Hammond homeowners and businesses bring up most before a drainage project. If your question is not answered here, call us or request a free quote, and we will talk it through when we come out to look at the water on your property.

What are signs of poor drainage in my yard?

Water pooling in the yard, a lawn that stays waterlogged after heavy rain, erosion and mold growth near the house are the usual signs that your Hammond landscape needs better drainage.

How does a French drain work?

A French drain is a trench filled with gravel or rock with a perforated pipe at the bottom. Water flows into the trench and is diverted away from the property.

Where are channel drains installed?

Channel drains go where excess water needs managing on hard surfaces, such as driveways, patios and sidewalks. Rainwater flows into the grate and the pipe below leads it away.

When does a dry well make sense?

Dry wells are typically used where the soil drains poorly or the area is prone to flooding. They collect excess water underground and let it infiltrate slowly into the ground.

How does a sump pump work in a landscape?

The pump sits in a sump pit where water collects. When the water level reaches a certain point, the pump activates and moves the water away.

Can clogged gutters cause drainage problems?

Yes. Clogged gutters and downspouts stop water from flowing away from the property, so it pools around the foundation or damages the outside of the building.

Does drainage help my lawn and plants?

Yes. Proper drainage prevents waterlogged soil and root rot, which gives your plants and lawn a healthier place to grow.

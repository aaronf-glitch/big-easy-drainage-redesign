Drainage services

Who Installs Drainage Systems in New Orleans, LA?

At Big Easy Drainage, we install, build, repair and maintain catch basins, channel drains, dry wells, French drains and sump pumps across New Orleans. Tell us where the water collects, and we will match the right system to your property, starting with a free estimate.

Catch Basins

A grated collection box set in a low spot, where water gathers and leaves through a pipe sloped away from your property.

Fixes puddles in low spots, on driveways and in parking areas.

Catch basin installation

Channel Drains

A shallow trench with a removable grate that catches sheet flow on driveways, patios, pool areas and sidewalk edges.

Fixes sheet flow across driveways, patios and pool decks.

Channel drain installation

Dry Wells

An underground pit of gravel or crushed stone that takes in runoff and lets it soak slowly into the soil, where drainage is poor or the water table is high.

Fixes soil that will not drain and yards with a high water table.

Dry well installation

French Drains

A perforated pipe in a gravel-filled trench, sloped toward a drainage point, that catches water before it reaches your foundation.

Fixes water heading for your foundation or basement.

French drain installation

Sump Pumps

A pump in a sump pit at the lowest point of the home that moves water out and away, keeping basements and crawl spaces dry.

Fixes water in crawl spaces and basements.

Sump pump installation

Drainage Questions, Answered

How can I request a consultation for my drainage issue?

Fill out our online form or give us a call at (504) 285-9658. We will schedule a convenient time to assess your drainage concerns.

How do I know which drainage solution is right for my property?

Our experts assess your property's topography and drainage issues, then recommend the solution that suits your needs.

How long does a typical landscape drainage project take?

It depends on how complex the problem is and which solutions it needs. Simple fixes might take a day, while more involved projects could take several days.

Do you maintain drainage systems after installing them?

Yes. Our services include drainage repair and maintenance as well as installation. For channel drains, our aftercare includes routine maintenance and cleaning.

Will drainage work spoil the look of my landscape?

No. Our team designs drainage solutions to blend into your landscape and preserve its visual appeal.

How does landscape drainage affect property value?

Effective drainage can prevent costly damage to your foundation and landscaping. That helps maintain your property's value and can increase it.

How can I tell if I have drainage problems on my property?

Look for standing water, soggy patches, erosion or water collecting around the foundation. These signs can point to poor drainage.

What are the common causes of poor landscape drainage?

Poor grading, compacted soil, drainage systems that are inadequate or blocked, and improper planning are some common causes of drainage problems.

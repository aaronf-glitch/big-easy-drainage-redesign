Why it matters here

Why Does Hardscaping Matter for New Orleans Drainage?

Hard surfaces do not soak up rain the way soil does, so water runs off them and has to go somewhere. In a city that gets heavy rain and sits near sea level, that runoff can pool or head toward a foundation. Good hardscape planning includes a path for the water.

Runoff From Hard Surfaces

Rain runs off paving instead of soaking into the ground.

Pooling Risk

Low spots on a hard surface can hold water after a storm.

Foundation Protection

Water that runs toward a house can cause damage over time.

Slope and Direction

The tilt of a surface decides where the water ends up.

Upkeep

How Do You Look After Hardscape in New Orleans?

Hard surfaces need simple, regular care. Sweep off leaves and debris so water can move freely, and look for cracks, loose pieces or low spots where water stands after rain. Watch the edges for soil that washes out. If water collects or runs toward the house, call us.

Sweep Regularly

Leaves and dirt can block the way water leaves a surface.

Look for Low Spots

Puddles that stay after rain point to a slope problem.

Check the Edges

Soil washing out along an edge shows where water is moving.

What is hardscaping?

Hardscaping covers the built, non-plant parts of a landscape, such as patios, walkways and walls. It sits alongside the plants and lawn that make up the softscape. Together they shape how a yard looks and works.

How much do hardscape services cost?

Why does hardscaping affect drainage?

Paving and other hard surfaces do not absorb rain, so the water runs off. If the surface slopes toward a house or has low spots, water can pool and cause damage. Planning the slope and the outlet helps prevent that.

What is the difference between hardscape and softscape?

Hardscape is the built, non-plant part of a landscape, like patios and walls. Softscape is the living part, like lawn, shrubs and flower beds. A good yard plan considers both.

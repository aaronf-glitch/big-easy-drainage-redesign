Why it matters here

Why Do New Orleans Homes Need a French Drain?

New Orleans sits below sea level and is surrounded by water, so heavy rainfall and hurricanes often lead to flooding that damages homes and businesses. A French drain collects excess water underground and redirects it away from your property before it can reach the foundation.

Collects Excess Water

Water soaks through the gravel and into the perforated pipe instead of sitting on your yard.

Redirects It Away

The pipe is sloped toward a drainage point, so the water keeps moving off your property.

Protects the Foundation

Catching water before it reaches the foundation or basement prevents damage there.

Built to Last

Durable materials stand up to heavy rainfall and long exposure to moisture.

How we install it

How Do We Install a French Drain in New Orleans?

We map how water flows across your yard, dig a trench with a slight slope toward the drainage point and lay gravel bedding. Then we set the perforated pipe, cover it with more gravel or rock, and add a waterproof membrane where the foundation needs one.

Get Free QuoteCall (504) 285-9658

  1. Find the Water's Path

    We choose the drain location from the way water flows across your yard.

  2. Dig a Sloped Trench

    The trench runs with a slight slope toward the drainage point.

  3. Lay the Gravel Bedding

    A layer of gravel at the bottom of the trench helps water filter through.

  4. Set and Cover the Pipe

    We position the perforated pipe and cover it with more gravel or rock, which filters debris.

  5. Protect the Foundation

    Where it is needed, a waterproof membrane keeps moisture from seeping into your foundation.

Other drainage systems

Which Other Drainage Systems Do We Install in New Orleans?

A French drain is one of five systems we install. When water collects in a low spot, sheets across a driveway, will not soak into the soil or reaches a crawl space, another system may suit your property better, and we will tell you which one when we visit.

Catch Basins

A grated collection box set in a low spot, where water gathers and leaves through a pipe.

Catch basin installation

Channel Drains

A shallow trench with a removable grate that catches sheet flow on driveways, patios and pool areas.

Channel drain installation

Dry Wells

An underground pit of gravel or crushed stone that lets runoff soak slowly into the soil.

Dry well installation

French Drain Questions From New Orleans Homeowners

These are the questions we hear most before a French drain goes in. If yours is not here, call us or request a free quote and we will answer it when we look at your property.

What is a French drain?

A French drain is a trench with a perforated pipe surrounded by gravel or rock. Excess water flows into the pipe and is carried away from your home's foundation to a drainage point.

How much does a French drain cost in New Orleans?

The cost depends on factors such as the size of your yard and your soil conditions. We look at your property and give you a free estimate, so you know the cost before any work starts.

How is a French drain installed?

We find where water flows on your yard, dig a trench with a slight slope toward the drainage point and lay gravel bedding. Then we set the perforated pipe and cover it with more gravel or rock.

Can I install a French drain myself?

The steps are simple to describe, but placement, slope and soil decide whether the drain works. Consulting professionals is still the safer choice for a drain that handles the challenges of your property.

How do you maintain a French drain?

Remove debris and sediment that build up over time, using a shovel or a high-pressure water hose to flush the drain. Check for clogs or leaks and fix them promptly. We can handle the upkeep for you.

Do French drains help with flooding in New Orleans?

A French drain collects excess water and redirects it away from your property during heavy rain, before it reaches your foundation or basement. It is one part of keeping a New Orleans property dry.

Do you install French drains for businesses?

Yes. We install French drains for residential and commercial properties in New Orleans, designed around the drainage problem on each site.

About Madisonville, LA

Why Does Drainage Matter in Madisonville, LA?

Madisonville is a small St. Tammany Parish town on the Tchefuncte River. Storm surge from Lake Pontchartrain flooded much of the town during Hurricane Katrina in 2005, and it flooded again during Hurricane Isaac in 2012, so moving water off every yard matters.

Founded in 1800

Jean Baptiste Baham founded the town in 1800 as Coquille, for the shells found there.

Named for a President

It was renamed in honor of President James Madison around 1811.

River Port

Madisonville shipped bricks and other goods down the Tchefuncte River to New Orleans before the Civil War.

On the Water

  • Maritime Museum Louisiana hosts a Wooden Boat Festival each fall
  • The Tchefuncte River Lighthouse is on the National Register
  • The lighthouse can only be reached by boat

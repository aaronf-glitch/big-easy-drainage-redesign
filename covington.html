Drainage assessment

Why Do Covington Yards Need a Drainage Assessment?

Lush Covington landscapes can hide drainage problems. Improper drainage leads to soil erosion, water accumulation and even structural damage. A thorough assessment finds the weak spots and the places water collects, so the fix we install protects both the look and the life of your landscape.

Soil Erosion

Water moving across the yard carries soil away and wears down beds and slopes.

Water Accumulation

Low spots and poorly graded areas hold water after rain instead of draining it.

Structural Damage

Water that is not directed away can damage structures on your property over time.

About Covington, LA

Why Does Yard Drainage Matter in Covington, LA?

Covington sits on the North Shore of Lake Pontchartrain, at the fork of the Bogue Falaya and Tchefuncte rivers, and it is the parish seat of St. Tammany Parish. With that much water around the city, heavy rain needs a planned path away from your home and yard.

Parish Seat

Covington is the parish seat of St. Tammany Parish, on the North Shore of Lake Pontchartrain.

Two Rivers

The city sits at the fork of the Bogue Falaya and the Tchefuncte River.

Tammany Trace

A 31-mile rail-to-trail path for walking, cycling and skating runs through Covington.

How do I know if my landscape has drainage issues?

Signs of drainage problems include standing water, soil erosion, mold growth and uneven plant growth. If you notice these signs on your Covington property, have a drainage professional take a look.

How often should I have my landscape drainage system inspected?

We recommend an inspection at least once a year to catch potential issues before they escalate. Weather conditions and changes to your landscape can make more frequent checks worthwhile.

Can you repair an existing drainage system that's not functioning correctly?

Yes. Our team diagnoses and repairs drainage systems. We assess the issue and carry out the repairs that restore proper function.

Can landscape drainage solutions enhance my property value?

Yes. Well-designed and maintained drainage can improve the value of your property by protecting it from water damage and keeping the outdoor space appealing.

Do you install drainage in Covington, LA?

Which drainage system does my Covington yard need?
